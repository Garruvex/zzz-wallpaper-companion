Games

Add your own game files, then press Play on TV and the wallpaper switches to the game. Each game keeps its saves in its own folder (tray menu › Open game ROM folder), so copying the folder takes your progress with it. No games are included.

PS1: single-image games, BIOS-free compatibility varies. CUE/BIN sets and disc switching are not supported yet. PBP is classified as PS1. PSP uses the experimental EmulatorJS 4.3.0 prerelease with GPU rendering. GTA: Vice City Stories may hang during its intro. PSP requires a compatible wallpaper host; in-game saves download as a .zzzpsp bundle.
